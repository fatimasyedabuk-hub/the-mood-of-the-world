ABOUT

What if people could feel weather data, instead of just reading it?

Type a city name and its current weather is fetched live from Open-Meteo. The data does not sit beside the design, it shapes it. The readings are current conditions from a weather model, not from a physical sensor.

How the data shapes the scene
DataWhat changes
TemperatureColour: below 10°C cold blues, 10 to 24°C soft violet and blush, 25°C and over warm amber
Weather codePicture, rain or snow animation, message and ambient sound
Wind speedHow fast clouds and rain or snow move
Cloud coverHow many cloud shapes appear, and how dense they are
HumidityA soft haze over the scene
Day or nightBrightness, and the night sky

The thresholds are design choices, not official weather categories.
